Stoic — Regime-Aware Risk Overlay

A CoinMarketCap Skill that emits a backtestable Strategy Capsule for a regime-aware DIRECTIONAL (trend/momentum) core — gated by a CoinMarketCap Fear & Greed contrarian regime read and risk-filtered by a positioning/flow divergence overlay. It is a risk overlay / drawdown manager: on REAL multi-regime daily data it roughly halves maximum drawdown vs buy-and-hold across bull, bear and chop, and out-returns B&H out-of-sample specifically by sitting out the 2026 bear.

What this edge IS — and is NOT (stated up front)
The robust, regime-independent claim is DRAWDOWN REDUCTION — the overlay roughly halves max-DD vs buy-and-hold on every window/cost tested. The out-of-sample B&H beat is a BEAR-DODGE, not standalone alpha: the long-only core goes flat in bears (dodging the 2026 drawdown) but LAGS in bulls — in-sample (a strong bull) it returns +57.5% vs B&H +262.0%, and in a bull mid-window it loses to B&H by ~37%. Two of three OOS "beats" are negative-absolute "lost-less" beats. We never present the absolute beat as regime-independent out-performance. Source: ../backtest/report-momentum.json + ROBUSTNESS-momentum.md.
